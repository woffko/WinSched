## Representative tests

The native runner deliberately executes Windows process-policy tests in an
interactive elevated session and stops an installed WinSched service to avoid
named-pipe collisions:

```powershell
.\tests\windows\native-test-runner.ps1 `
  -TestDirectory C:\Users\Public\WinSchedNativeTests `
  -OutputDirectory C:\Users\Public\WinSchedNativeResults
```

The logging acceptance verifies three distinct levels end to end:

```powershell
.\tests\windows\logging-acceptance.ps1 `
  -InteractiveUser $interactiveUser
```

The UI-only orchestrator avoids repeating destructive service tests:

```powershell
.\tests\windows\ui-regression-acceptance.ps1 `
  -TestDirectory .\tests\windows `
  -InteractiveUser $interactiveUser `
  -OutputRoot C:\Users\Public\WinSchedUiEvidence
```

Scheduling state in `finally`, including failure paths:

## Evidence and limitations

Privacy-minimized structured receipts are under
`tests/evidence/runtime/v0.5.1/`. They omit transient PIDs, generated marker
values, and machine-specific output paths when those values do not establish a
product invariant.

The VM has two logical processors and two visible LLC domains. It proves
Windows execution, rollback, installation, and UI behavior; it does not prove
Threadripper performance. The physical 32-core/64-thread Auto/Disabled ABBA
experiment and any resulting Responsive-scope decision remain separate gates.

## Physical pre-ABBA results

The same Setup later upgraded the physical Threadripper host from 0.5.0 to
0.5.1 while preserving the schema-4 TOML byte-for-byte. Scheduling remained
enabled, legacy logging remained Off after in-memory schema-5 migration, all
four installed executable hashes matched the VM-accepted payload, the service
remained Running, and `last_error` remained empty.

With Logging Off, a 30-second sample reported 3.38% of one core (0.0529% of
machine capacity), zero file-log records/bytes/errors, and byte-stable retained
log files. With Normal temporarily enabled for 75 seconds, the service emitted
9 records / 4,601 bytes, including one complete minute summary, two raw mutation
decisions, zero raw no-op decisions, and zero write errors. The exact original
Off configuration was restored afterward.

The first completed physical Auto/Disabled ABBA used manual F11/F12 endpoints.
It is invalid for policy because it included human reaction time, the two pair
directions disagreed, and its Disabled phases were confounded by a zero-wait
service loop. Those phases used 35.62-40.57% of one core and performed
27,768-31,443 writes. The defect was fixed and a Windows VM candidate run then
measured 0% of one core, three writes, three status heartbeats, and zero policy
evaluations over 30.006 seconds. The exact original VM binary, configuration,
service state, and Scheduling state were restored. A passive physical taskbar
ABBA remains pending. Before that full run, a one-attempt physical passive
marker pilot passed: all three physical clicks were recognized as taskbar
clicks, one minimize was independently confirmed, and the accepted restore took
156 ms to foreground plus 31 ms to a responsive message pump, 187 ms total.
After installing the corrected Setup on the physical host, a 30.006-second
Disabled-idle gate measured 0.208% of one core, three writes (0.10/s), three
status heartbeats, and zero policy evaluations. The service PID remained stable
and Scheduling was restored to Enabled.
